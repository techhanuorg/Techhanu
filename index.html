Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before initiating a project or retainer with TechHanu.

Who founded TechHanu and when?

TechHanu was founded in 2024 by Nitin Singh. We are an Indian digital engineering team specializing in bespoke website development, web applications, and WhatsApp AI business automations.

How does the 20% advance payment policy work?

You pay 20% advance upfront to book engineering capacity and commence development. We build the solution, present progress demonstrations in staging, and you pay the remaining 80% balance only after you review and approve the final work.

What is the typical delivery timeline for a website?

Standard business websites are typically delivered within 7 to 14 business days, provided branding assets and content are shared promptly. Custom web applications and e-commerce portals follow project-specific milestone schedules.

What is a WhatsApp AI Agent and how does it help my business?

It is an automated customer communication assistant running directly on your official WhatsApp Business number. It responds to customer questions 24/7, qualifies leads, captures contact info into Google Sheets, and alerts human staff when personal attention is needed.

Are WhatsApp API and server hosting costs included?

No. Third-party expenses (Meta WhatsApp API conversation fees, domain registration, and cloud hosting) are paid directly to those respective service providers. Our fees cover design, engineering, implementation, and ongoing management.

How can I reach the TechHanu founders directly?
