Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

TechHanu ("we", "us", or "our") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect when you visit techhanu.io, make a project enquiry, or communicate with our team, and how that information is handled, stored, and protected.

Key Commitment: TechHanu does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to evaluate project requirements, provide price quotations, and deliver agreed digital engineering services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Details: Your full name, business or organization name, email address, phone number, and WhatsApp number when you submit an enquiry form or message us.
  • Project Specifications: Details about your required services (such as website development, web applications, mobile apps, or WhatsApp automation), anticipated project scope, and custom messages.
  • Direct Communications: Information exchanged over WhatsApp, email, or telephone during consultations and project delivery.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Hosting and Server Logs: When you access this website, standard technical request headers (such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and access timestamps) may be automatically logged by our static hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) for security monitoring and operational integrity.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use lightweight browser storage (such as localStorage) solely for functional anti-spam rate limiting (for example, preventing duplicate form submissions within a 10-second interval). We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your project enquiries and provide accurate scope assessments and cost proposals.
  • To communicate project milestones, updates, design revisions, and support notifications.
  • To maintain internal project records, accounting documentation, and service logs.
  • To prevent automated bot abuse, spam submissions, and malicious activity on our forms.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations under Indian law.

3. Third-Party Integrations and Processing

To operate our public website and enquiry workflow efficiently, we utilize select third-party service providers who process data in accordance with their respective privacy terms:

  • Google Apps Script & Google Sheets (Google LLC): Used as our secure data ingestion backend for recording submitted enquiries. Form submissions are transmitted via HTTPS to Google cloud endpoints and appended to an access-controlled Google Sheet managed by TechHanu.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft): Provides the static web hosting infrastructure delivering our HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and image assets.
  • WhatsApp / Meta Platforms: When you choose to contact us or receive project updates via WhatsApp, conversations are subject to WhatsApp's standard end-to-end encryption and terms of service. Meta API charges for automation bots are billed under the client's direct Meta account or as outlined in specific project contracts.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain enquiry information only as long as necessary to fulfill project evaluation, contract execution, accounting record-keeping, and customer service follow-ups. If you submit an enquiry that does not result in an active project engagement, you may request complete deletion of your contact records at any time by writing to us at techhanu.io@gmail.com. We will process verified requests within 30 business days.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards:

  • All web traffic between your browser and our website is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS).
  • Form inputs undergo client-side validation and server-side sanitization to protect against cross-site scripting (XSS) and injection vulnerabilities.
  • Access to internal customer records and project sheets is restricted strictly to authorized founders and team members using multi-factor authentication.

6. Indian Privacy Framework and User Rights

TechHanu operates from India and adheres to fair digital data practices consistent with the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework as applicable. As a user, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold personal data relating to you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Request the erasure of your personal data where retention is no longer necessary for contractual or legal purposes.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.
